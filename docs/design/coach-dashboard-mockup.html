What changes and why

Today stays first: the next session big, the rest as a strip, and a flag if attendance is waiting to be marked.

To do becomes one list ordered by urgency and driven by every feature in the app: messages, new members, check-ins, meal photos, scores, forms, deletion requests. Each row has a count and goes straight to the right place. Empty rows disappear; an empty list says "All clear".

Members to check in on replaces the plain "gone quiet" rows with cards that say why: quiet, missed check-ins, low credits, weight stalled.

This week gives the coach (and manager) the pulse in four numbers, with a link through to Reports.

Quick add (+): add a client, copy the sign-up link, or send a broadcast, from anywhere on the dashboard.

Recent activity drops to the bottom and shrinks to four lines; it is nice to have, not the first thing a coach needs.
