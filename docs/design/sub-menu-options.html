Menu options inside each screen

Saved 8 Oct 2026. The bottom bar stays as it is. This page is about the second level: how someone moves between the parts of one main screen. Today everything is a pill, whether it switches a view, picks a day or filters a list. The mockups below show the alternatives on the real main screens (Coach, Book and Profile). Static mockups, not wired into any page. Nothing in the app has changed.

  • Suggested rule: tabs move you to a different section, chips filter a list, a stepper or strip picks a day or week. Using pills for all three is why some screens feel inconsistent.
  • Section switching on Coach is the clearest problem: six sections do not fit as pills. Options 2 (underline tabs) or 4 (tile menu) fit best.
  • For Book, option 3 (segmented) fits the three views and option 12 (date strip) fits the days. Profile reads best as option 8 (settings list).
