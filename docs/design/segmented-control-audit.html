Segmented control audit

Saved 8 Oct 2026. We searched the whole app for pills. This page shows every place where the options are 2 or 3 and the pills switch a view or set a value. Each is shown as it is now (pills) beside the same screen with a segmented control. Static mockups with the real labels. Nothing in the app has changed.

FindingDetail
Screen switchers are almost all 2–3Member Nutrition, Training, Accountability, Progress and the Book switch all have 2–3 options.
Already underline tabsThe coach hubs: Classes (3), Messages (2), Library (4), Business (5–6), plus the tab row when a coach opens a client (up to 5). The segmented control would not fit Library, Business or the client tabs, so they stay as they are.
Already segmentedMember Book (Book a session / My bookings) and three pickers in Coach Broadcasts.
Hand-built look-alikesAt least 8 places rebuild the segmented look by hand (Reports, Timetable kind, Clients scope, water unit, kg/cm toggles), so they would share one component.
Not suitableFilters with 5–9 options and day, week and weekday pickers (listed at the bottom). They stay chips or strips.

3. Not suitable: these stay as they are

WhereOptionsWhy not segmented
Coach > Library hubExercises, Program templates, Education, Forms4 long labels; already underline tabs
Coach > Business hubOverview, Plans, Credit packs, Events, Rewards, Feedback5–6 items; already underline tabs
Coach viewing a clientUp to 5 per section (Summary, Insights, Photos, Body scans, Big Dog)Up to 5; already underline tabs
Coach > Classes and Messages hubs3 and 2 optionsCould be segmented, but they are page-level tabs like the other hubs, so they match Library and Business as underline tabs
Member > Training > Activity filterAll, Walking, Running, Cycling, Swimming, Gym6 filter options
Coach > Clients filterAll, New to review, Gone quiet, Low credits, No plan5 filter options with counts
Exercise filters (3 places)All + 7–8 muscle groups8–9 filter options
Booking and check-in day stripsAbout 14 dates, or 7 daysDates are a scroll strip, not a switch
Week pickerWeek 1 to NUp to 12 weeks, scrolls
Weekday chipsMon–Sat or Mon–Sun, several can be pickedMulti-select, 6–7 options
Ratings and cards1–10 effort, 5 stars, mood dots, onboarding option cards, form answersDifferent controls with their own jobs
  • The rule that falls out of the audit: 2–3 options that switch a view or set a value use a segmented control. 4 or more options for a page use underline tabs. Filters with many options use chips. Dates, weeks and weekdays use strips.
  • The Progress mockup uses the longest label. On a 360px phone, "Progress & Photos" may wrap or shrink, so it needs checking on a real phone if segmented is chosen.
  • The audit searched by pattern rather than reading every line of every file, so a stray pill group could still exist.
  • Not decided yet. Nothing in the app has changed.
