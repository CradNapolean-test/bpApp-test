Segmented design options

Saved 8 Oct 2026. Twelve ways to style the same control. Each shows the three-option case with the longest label in the app ("Progress & Photos") and a two-option case. Click any control to switch it. Static mockups, not wired into any page. Nothing in the app has changed.

OptionFits 3 long labelsFeels
1, 2, 3, 8, 9Yes, about the sameiOS / Android-style switch. Differences are only in the selected look.
4 Soft tintYesQuieter, less shouty on a screen with a lot of accent colour.
5 SquareYes, a bit more roomSharp, "tool" look. Clashes with the rounded cards.
6 Joined buttonsYesReads as buttons rather than a switch.
7 Underline in columnsYes, most roomSame family as the coach's underline tabs, so one language across the app.
10 With iconsTighter, labels must be shortFriendly, easy to scan; needs an icon for every option.
11 Separate chipsYesClosest to pills but equal width, so it still reads as a choice of one.
12 With countsTighterUseful for "My bookings (2)", "Unread (3)".
  • The app's shared segmented control today is closest to option 2 (rounded track with an outline, accent fill). The earlier comparison pages used option 1.
  • Any of these works in light and dark mode; the colours here are dark mode only.
  • Option 7 is the one to look at if the owners want the segmented screens and the coach's underline tabs to look related.
