  • These are not exclusive. A real app usually mixes one main menu (1–4, 11–13, 15) with an add-on (7 avatar menu, 10 action button, 16 search).
  • The app uses option 1 for members on a phone, option 11 for members on desktop, and options 3 and 13 for the coach. Nothing is decided; this is for the owners to react to.
  • Questions to put to the owners: how many main areas will members need in a year? Is there one action members do most (book a class, log food)? Should the coach and member apps feel the same?
  • Suggested starting point: keep 1 for members (it works best at four destinations), and keep 13 for the coach, with 16 added once the client list grows.
