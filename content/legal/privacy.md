## What we collect

- **You and how to reach you:** name, email, phone number, date of birth, gender, emergency contact, timezone.
- **Health and body:** health notes, injuries and limitations, questionnaire and intake answers, weight, measurements, body-scan results and printouts, progress photos, sleep, steps, water, mood ratings, calorie targets.
- **Training and food:** food diary, meal photos, programmes, workout logs, challenge results.
- **Gym activity:** class bookings and attendance, credits, plans, events, feedback.
- **Messages:** chat messages, voice notes, photos, broadcasts and emails to your coach.
- **Coach records:** notes your coach keeps about you, and follow-ups when you have been quiet.
- **Your device:** the notification address of your browser or phone, whether the app is installed, sign-in sessions, and server logs that include your network address.

## Why we use it

To run your coaching: set your targets, plan your training, take bookings, let your coach see how you are doing and message you, and keep the gym running. Health information is used only for that, and only with your agreement, which you give when you set up your account. (Add the legal basis here.)

## Who can see it

- **Your coach** sees your profile, health details, check-ins, food, training, photos, body scans, forms, messages, and the notes they keep about you.
- **Other coaches at your gym** (every coach who works at the gym you belong to, not only your own coach) can read most of the same information. The two lists below say exactly what they can and cannot see.
- **Gym administrators** are coaches with extra rights at your gym, so they can read what any coach at your gym can read.
- **The gym owner** sees membership and attendance figures, activity figures (for example how many check-ins and messages there were, not what they say), and lists of members with their name, email, coach, plan and status. Your health details, diary and messages are not shown to the owner. If the owner also coaches at your gym, they can read what a coach at your gym can read.
- **No one outside the gym** sees it, except the services below that run the app for us.

### What other coaches at your gym can read

- **Your profile:** name, email, phone number, address, date of birth, gender, emergency contact, age, height, starting and goal weight, measurements and lifts, your goals, and your health notes.
- **Check-ins:** your daily logs (weight, sleep, steps, water, calories and macros, hunger, energy, motivation and stress ratings, period tracking, and notes) and your body measurements.
- **Food:** your food diary, calories and macros you add by hand, meal plans, recipes, saved foods, and the details of each meal photo entry (the meal, your description and the estimated calories), but not the photo itself.
- **Training:** your programmes, workout logs, feedback on your workouts, and your tested maxes.
- **Forms:** the forms and questionnaires sent to you and your answers, including intake and health questions.
- **Habits and courses:** your habits and the days you ticked them off, and the courses and lessons assigned to you and completed.
- **Gym activity:** class bookings and attendance, credit history, membership plans and holds, event sign-ups, groups, rewards and challenge shirts, feedback you send the gym, and weekly attendance follow-ups (classes attended, late cancellations, no-shows, and whether and why you were contacted).
- **Coach records:** the notes and journal entries coaches keep about you (including notes about injuries and goals), and which of your check-ins, diary days and forms a coach has marked as reviewed.
- **Other:** the notifications the app sends you (reminders and alerts), which app screens are switched off for you, and the dates you added progress photos.

### What other coaches at your gym cannot see

- Your chat with your coach, including the photos and voice notes in it. Your messages are seen only by you and your coach.
- Your meal photos and progress photos themselves.
- Your body-scan results and printouts, your challenge results, and your coach's comments on your meal photos.
- Anything about you, if they only coach at a different gym.

### Help signing in

Your coach, or an administrator at your gym, can create a link that sets a new password for your account, for example if you are locked out. The link is emailed to you and is also shown to the person who made it. Anyone who opens the link can sign in to your account.

## Your choices and rights

- **See your data:** use "Download my data" in your Account screen for a copy as a file.
- **Delete your account:** use "Delete my account" in your Account screen. Your profile, logs, photos, messages and bookings are deleted straight away. This does not cancel your membership: tell the gym.
- **Stop emails:** use the link at the bottom of any broadcast email, or switch emails off in your Account screen.
- **Stop notifications:** switch them off in your Account screen or in your phone settings.
- **Correct something:** change it in the app, or ask your coach.
